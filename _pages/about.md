I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, a member of the **HKUST NLP Group**, advised by **Professor Junxian He**.

I received my **B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024, where Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies.

My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**, including the following topics:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**

I have been a Research Intern at **MINIMAX**, **Tencent WXG**, and **Shanghai AI Lab**. See my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=tbK9jl4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) and my [Publications page]({{ "/publications/" | relative_url }}) for details.
